# Just Roll Once
## Game Directions
1. Roll the dice to earn money.
2. The amount earned depends on the dice values rolled and the player stats.
3. Spend money in the shop to purchase upgrades.
4. Upgrades increase your earning potential.
5. Continue upgrading and rolling to earn more money.
6. Discover all of the achievements.

## Motivation & Inspiration
- Cookies Clickers
- general incremental/idle games.

Designed and developed as part of UIC CS 141, Program Design II, in the Summer 2026 offering with Professor Koehler.
